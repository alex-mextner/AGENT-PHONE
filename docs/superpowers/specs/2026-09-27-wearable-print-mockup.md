# Complete wearable inert print study

This implements the owner's request for a mass-and-size mockup that can be
printed and worn without repeating the rejected orientation and end-hook
errors. It supersedes the earlier unwearable screen-block kit for wearable
trials, without deleting that earlier size-comparison artifact.

The module remains 92 mm along the forearm, 44 mm across and 6.6 mm thick.
The complete nominal closed top is 14.8 mm above modeled dorsal skin. A
fixed-closed sandwich replaces the working tilt mechanism for this inert
trial only: base, two central TPU flanges, ballast tray, flush removable lid.
Four recessed M3 through-bolts and nuts retain the assembly. A separate TPU
pad covers the dorsal nut pockets; no rigid block crosses the wrist underside.

The visible cuff blades are 12 mm wide at the neck/tip and 28 mm in the
curved middle, with approximately 3 mm radial thickness. Hidden flanges
have two bolts per side. A common flat axial face gives a deliberate
asymmetric taper for side printing; the supports remain central rather
than moving to the longitudinal module ends.

Three nominal wrist profiles and full C-shaped gauges are provided because
no actual wrist measurements were supplied. Gauge fit does not establish
full-blade stiffness or retention. A retained, deburred four-hole steel
insert has modeled capacity 55.9 g; the full assembly must be weighed.

Acceptance is exported-solid and STL round-trip checks, actual Boolean
assembly/nominal-wrist interference checks, negative controls, hard mesh
and wall-thickness gates, reloaded material-separated 3MF layouts, real
configured offline slicing, and inspected Blender renders of those exports.
Physical printing, hardware tolerances, comfort, fatigue, retention and
cold operation remain untested. Start with an unloaded, removable fit gauge.
