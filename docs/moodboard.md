# AGENT-PHONE visual moodboard

This board is for design decisions, not for copying another product. Images are loaded from their original/public hosts where possible; the repository does **not** redistribute those source files. Follow the source link for ownership and context.

The product baseline is: a wrist computer approximately the area of **two Apple Watch Ultra cases side by side**, one uninterrupted almost-borderless OLED, open cuff, hidden tilt mechanism, and no rigid block under the wrist.

## Film & game wrist computers

### REF-01 — Star Citizen mobiGlas

![Star Citizen mobiGlas](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/v2/t%3A0%2Cl%3A118%2Ccw%3A700%2Cch%3A525%2Cq%3A80%2Cw%3A700/CCMTsUzeTD4kqbnyVBDSx4.jpg)

**Source:** https://robertsspaceindustries.com/en/comm-link/engineering/14466-Design-Notes-MobiGlas

**KEEP:** diegetic personal-computer identity, contextual information, device-to-environment transfer, wide/panoramic information layouts.

**AVOID:** giant public holograms, forearm-scale hardware, always-visible luminous UI.

**WHY:** mobiGlas is useful less as a shape reference than as a model of a personal computer that can move information between the wrist, AR and nearby screens.

### REF-02 — Mass Effect / Andromeda UI

![Mass Effect Andromeda UI](https://mir-s3-cdn-cf.behance.net/project_modules/1400/818d2811430497.560f79c8260ad.jpg)

**Source:** https://www.behance.net/gallery/51952829/Mass-Effect-Andromeda-User-Interface-Design

**KEEP:** contextual tools, compact visual hierarchy, fast switching between data and world context.

**AVOID:** decorative sci-fi chrome, permanent AR clutter, interfaces that require a huge projected plane.

**WHY:** it demonstrates how a futuristic interface can remain structured and readable instead of becoming a wall of glowing widgets.

### REF-03 — Fallout Pip-Boy

**Source:** https://fallout.bethesda.net/en-US/news/fallout-pip-boy-3000-replica

**KEEP:** unmistakable “computer worn on the body” identity and confidence that the object is a primary computer rather than a notification watch.

**AVOID:** bulk, forearm placement, fixed viewing angle, physical controls surrounding the screen.

**WHY:** AGENT-PHONE should inherit the category confidence without inheriting Pip-Boy ergonomics.

## Real wearables

### REF-04 — Nubia Alpha

![Nubia Alpha](https://scr3.golem.de/screenshots/1902/Nubia_Alpha_Hands_on/thumb620/P2322030.jpg)

**Source:** https://www.golem.de/news/nubia-alpha-im-hands-on-flexibles-display-macht-die-smartwatch-gross-und-dick-1902-139657.html

**KEEP:** proof that an elongated flexible OLED can live on a wrist; screen/body curvature that follows anatomy.

**AVOID:** a narrow portrait strip, thick side housings, phone-app-list UI wrapped around the arm.

**WHY:** it validates display packaging, but AGENT-PHONE uses a wider landscape information surface and a separate open-cuff battery structure.

### REF-05 — Meta Ray-Ban Display + Neural Band

**Source:** https://about.fb.com/news/2025/09/meta-ray-ban-display-ai-glasses-emg-wristband/

**KEEP:** short glanceable interactions, display that is present only when needed, subtle sEMG finger input, glasses + wrist input as one system.

**AVOID:** requiring a second dedicated wristband when AGENT-PHONE already occupies the wrist; locking interaction to one eyewear vendor.

**WHY:** this is the strongest current consumer proof that tiny hand movements and a low-duty-cycle glasses display can reduce dependence on a phone screen.

## Open cuffs & tilting displays

### REF-06 — WristPC concept (Novate reference supplied by the user)

![WristPC tilting screen](https://novate.ru/files/u18226/wristpc-ultra-mobile-personal-computer2.jpg)

**Source:** https://novate.ru/blogs/240711/18254/

**KEEP:** **open-bottom cuff** and a screen that physically tilts toward the user instead of forcing wrist rotation.

**AVOID:** keypad-heavy bracelet, 3.5-inch phone-scale display, thick exposed hinge, game controls, general 2011 industrial styling.

**WHY:** this is the closest direct precedent for the two mechanical ideas the user independently arrived at. Everything else should be substantially thinner and cleaner.

### REF-07 — Motorola pivoting wearable concept

![Motorola pivoting wearable](https://cdn.trendhunterstatic.com/thumbs/625/motorola-pivoting-wearable.jpeg)

**Source:** https://www.trendhunter.com/trends/motorola-pivoting-wearable

**KEEP:** reconfigurable viewing angle and the idea that a wearable display can have more than one useful physical state.

**AVOID:** making the whole band a complex moving display or creating a fragile multi-link mechanism.

**WHY:** it reinforces the value of physical reconfiguration while showing why AGENT-PHONE should keep the hinge local and mechanically simple.

### REF-08 — INFINITY touch bracelet

![INFINITY touch bracelet](https://mir-s3-cdn-cf.behance.net/project_modules/disp/57d83e31231415.564d93b73553d.jpg)

**Source:** https://www.behance.net/gallery/31231415/Touch-bracelet-INFINITY

**KEEP:** continuous, jewelry-like cuff curvature and clean transitions from rigid electronics to the wrist.

**AVOID:** rigidly enclosing the entire circumference and using the whole bracelet as screen.

**WHY:** useful for silhouette and proportion studies of an open cuff that looks intentional rather than like two battery blocks.

### REF-09 — Sugar pivoting bracelet concept

**Source:** https://www.behance.net/gallery/17612649/Sugar

**KEEP:** display moves relative to the body instead of requiring the user to move the whole arm.

**AVOID:** bulky pivot hardware and decorative shapes that make the mechanism the visual focus.

**WHY:** reinforces the “display moves, wrist stays relaxed” principle.

## Flexible / emissive displays

### REF-10 — FlexEnable bracelet display prototype

![FlexEnable bracelet display](https://www.infobae.com/resizer/v2/GRBJ7XBNVRHEBIXD3B2OLJT7XI?auth=193a53a4d57c498eed31cebb141fb165f795173ca9a53f54ac92be503a9329ba&height=900&quality=85&smart=true&width=1200)

**Source:** https://www.infobae.com/2016/02/25/1792717-el-celular-tendra-forma-pulsera/

**KEEP:** screen substrate conforming to a wrist-scale curve and very thin display stack.

**AVOID:** wrapping the active UI around a high-curvature side where text becomes difficult to read.

**WHY:** validates thin flexible display construction; AGENT-PHONE instead keeps its main reading surface nearly flat and uses curves for cuff/battery packaging.

### REF-11 — FlexEnable flexible OLCD wearable

![Flexible OLCD wearable](https://mv-image.moov.ooo/20200512102756_iidolf93kk8.jpg)

**Source:** https://moov.ooo/article/5eb9fba11b05ee0697dd2c97

**KEEP:** large information area on a wrist and a thin display body.

**AVOID:** very wide rigid metal side rails and an interface that assumes the display itself should wrap around the wrist.

**WHY:** a strong negative/positive reference for balancing screen area against wrist bulk.

### REF-12 — DuPont curved OLED concepts

**Source:** https://www.behance.net/gallery/18236253/Dupont-Displays-Curved-OLED-Display-Concepts

**KEEP:** smooth panel-to-body transitions and the possibility of curving only non-critical visual edges.

**AVOID:** treating curvature as decoration when a flat reading plane is ergonomically better.

**WHY:** informs cover-glass and body transitions around an almost-borderless OLED.

## Rings & spatial control

### REF-13 — IRIS camera smart ring

**Source:** https://www.washington.edu/news/2025/01/08/smart-ring-camera-iris/

**KEEP:** point at a physical device, click/confirm, rotate the hand for continuous control; ring as a context-aware spatial selector.

**AVOID:** making a ring camera mandatory for every interaction or continuously streaming room imagery.

**WHY:** IRIS directly validates the user’s “point at TV/light/speaker and manipulate it” interaction idea.

### REF-14 — Meta Neural Band gesture vocabulary

**Source:** https://about.fb.com/news/2025/09/meta-ray-ban-display-ai-glasses-emg-wristband/

**KEEP:** thumb/finger micro-gestures, wrist rotation as a dial, silent interaction with a display elsewhere.

**AVOID:** duplicating wrist hardware; the sensing should migrate into AGENT-PHONE and/or a ring.

**WHY:** current consumer implementation provides a useful interaction-quality benchmark for Telekinesis.

## Glasses

### REF-15 — Apple Vision Pro gaze + pinch

**Source:** https://support.apple.com/guide/apple-vision-pro/enter-text-and-use-dictation-tana14220eef/visionos

**KEEP:** gaze establishes the target; a small deliberate hand gesture confirms the action.

**AVOID:** requiring headset-scale hardware, persistent immersive UI, or gaze-only commits with no explicit confirmation.

**WHY:** a good interaction reference for optional Agent Glasses: eyes point, ring/hand confirms.

### REF-16 — Meta Ray-Ban Display

**Source:** https://about.fb.com/news/2025/09/meta-ray-ban-display-ai-glasses-emg-wristband/

**KEEP:** display off by default, short private visual answers, photo preview/navigation without pulling out a phone.

**AVOID:** moving the whole product experience into glasses; AGENT-PHONE must remain independently useful.

**WHY:** supports the product thesis that some wrist screen-on time can disappear entirely when a tiny glasses display is present.

## Detachable cameras

### REF-17 — Insta360 GO 3S

**Source:** https://www.insta360.com/blog/news/meet-insta360-go-3s-tiny-mighty-4k-camera.html

**KEEP:** tiny standalone magnetic camera body, wearable/remote mounting, separation of capture sensor from the main display.

**AVOID:** 39 g action-camera bulk as the final target and a second large “Action Pod” UI.

**WHY:** a strong physical precedent for Agent Camera: the sensor can detach and borrow viewfinder/control/compute from the wrist or glasses.

---

## Design synthesis

The recurring pattern is not “make a better smartwatch.” The strongest references split into four roles:

1. **Wrist computer:** a wide, physical, private information surface.
2. **Open cuff:** battery/sensors distributed around the wrist without a hard underside brick.
3. **Telekinesis / ring:** silent eyes-free input and spatial target selection.
4. **Glasses / camera tile / nearby screens:** move pixels and capture away from the wrist when that is ergonomically better.

The render baseline therefore rejects forearm computers, full-circumference screens, large exposed hinges, phone-style portrait app grids, and bulky camera hardware on the wrist.
